Midwifery

Compassionate Care.

Nurturing New Beginnings

Maternal Care

Specialized training in pre-natal, natal, and post-natal healthcare services.

Infant Health

Ensuring the safety and well-being of newborns through expert clinical practice.

Community Health

Promoting family planning and primary health care in local communities.

The Midwifery Program at the University of Eastern Pangasinan is committed to developing highly skilled health professionals who specialize in maternal and child care. We prepare our students to become vital partners in the healthcare system, focusing on safe childbirth and reproductive health.

Through rigorous clinical instruction and community immersion, students gain mastery in obstetric procedures and newborn care. We instill the values of patience, empathy, and clinical excellence required to serve mothers and infants with the utmost professional standard.

"Championing safe motherhood and a healthy future for every child."
